      The whole course, on one card

      Pot odds — what a call needs

      needed equity = call / (pot + call + call)

      Villain bets 50 into 100. You pay 50 to win 150, so you need 50/200 = 25%. Your own call sits in the denominator — that is the step people fluff.

      Bluff break-even — how often they must fold

      fold % needed = bet / (pot + bet)

      Half pot needs 33%. Pot needs 50%. Note your own bet is NOT in what you win here — you take the pot as it stands.

      Minimum defence frequency

      defend = pot / (pot + bet)

      Fold more than that and any two cards beats you. A ceiling on folding, not permission to call forever.

      Outs, and the rule of 2 and 4

      flop → outs × 4 turn → outs × 2

      Flush draw 9. Open-ended straight 8. Gutshot 4. Two overcards 6. Flush + gutshot 12. Past about eight outs the rule of 4 flatters you — knock a couple of points off.

      Implied odds

      extra needed = call / equity − (pot + call)

      Only real if the stacks are deep enough AND they will actually pay. Against someone who folds every river, implied odds are a bedtime story.

      EV of a call

      EV = equity × (pot + call) − (1 − equity) × call

      Positive means call, even when it loses this time. Especially when it loses this time.
