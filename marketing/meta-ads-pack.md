---

## ⚠️ READ THIS FIRST — Meta Policy Reality

This product touches crypto trading. Meta enforces these hard:

1. **Cryptocurrency Products & Services policy** — ads promoting crypto exchanges, trading, or related tools generally require **prior written permission / onboarding approval from Meta** before they'll run. Apply via Meta's crypto advertiser process *before* launching. ([Check current status in Ads Manager → Account Quality.])
2. **No income or return claims.** Never imply users will make money, "earn," "profit," "$X/day," or that results are likely. This is the #1 cause of rejection + account restriction.
3. **Do NOT use the "$1,000 BitGet bonus" as an ad hook.** Financial incentive + crypto = near-certain rejection. Keep affiliate offers on the destination page, not in the ad.
4. **No "get rich / quit your job" framing.** Keep it educational: AI, automation, open-source tooling.
5. **Personal attributes rule** — don't address the viewer's financial situation ("Struggling to trade?"). Keep copy product-focused, not person-focused.

**Safest positioning (used below):** "A free, open-source AI automation tutorial." That is 100% true and the least likely to get pulled.

Disclaimer to keep on the landing page (not needed in-ad, but good practice): *Not financial advice. For educational purposes. Trading involves risk of loss.*

# A/B Testing Plan

1. **Launch:** 2–3 ads per ad set, identical budget, run 4–7 days before judging.
2. **Primary metric:** Cost per landing-page view (or CPC). For content offers, watch **CTR** and **thumb-stop rate** (3s video views ÷ impressions).
3. **Don't kill ads on day 1** — Meta's learning phase needs ~50 optimization events per ad set.
4. **Iterate on the hook (first line of primary text)** — that's the highest-leverage variable. Creative (the video itself) is #2.
5. **Winner scaling:** duplicate the winning ad into a new Advantage+ ad set and raise budget gradually (≤20% increases) to avoid resetting learning.
